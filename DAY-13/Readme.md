
The EDA shows that restaurant performance varies across branches,
regions, and store types. Revenue, customer volume, orders, average
bill value, food cost, and operating cost are important variables
associated with profitability. Branch-level and regional comparisons
help identify stronger and weaker performers. Distribution plots reveal
the spread of customers, revenue, profit, marketing expenditure,
delivery time, and customer ratings. The correlation matrix provides
a quantitative view of relationships between numerical variables.
Overall, the analysis can help management identify high-performing
branches, understand major revenue and profit drivers, and improve
operational efficiency.
